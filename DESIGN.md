# Design System: BEM FISIP Unpad 2026

## Overview

This is a constraint seed, not a claim that the final visual system has been implemented. The client-provided 2026 Figma file, design system, and approved source assets outrank this document when they provide a more specific answer. The current Create Next App screen is not design authority.

The creative north star, exact composition, graphic language, density, imagery treatment, and motion grammar remain pending complete Figma inspect access and client confirmation. Future work may build approved primitives that are unaffected by those gaps, but it must not invent a competing identity.

**Key Characteristics:**

- Official, trustworthy, and clearly attributable to BEM FISIP Unpad 2026.
- Indonesian-first and resilient to long names, titles, and editorial content.
- Deliberately responsive, accessible, and useful before optional motion is applied.
- Faithful to the supplied 2026 design system rather than the inherited 2025 implementation or starter scaffold.

## Colors

The approved brand foundation is duotone: brand blue (`#003089`) and brand yellow (`#FFBA3A`). These values are fixed inputs; their exact distribution and derived tonal scales must be taken from Figma or established as named implementation tokens in an approved task.

White, black, neutral surfaces, and semantic status colors may support content and accessibility, but they do not become additional brand colors. Do not introduce unrelated gradients, decorative hues, or arbitrary opacity variants.

**The Contrast-Before-Brand Rule.** White on brand yellow does not meet normal-text contrast. Prefer brand blue on yellow where that pairing fits the approved design, and test every text, icon, focus, disabled, and interactive-state pairing against WCAG 2.2 AA.

## Typography

Inter is the primary and default typeface. Use one shared font token and intentional weight/size roles rather than introducing a display font or local one-off stacks.

Exact display, heading, body, and label sizes, weights, line heights, and tracking remain pending Figma inspection. Until confirmed, typography work must preserve readable line length, robust Indonesian text wrapping, and zoom behavior without presenting provisional measurements as final tokens.

**The One-Family Rule.** Do not add a competing brand typeface without explicit client approval.

## Layout

Desktop, tablet, and mobile compositions must be designed intentionally rather than produced by scaling down one desktop canvas. Exact grids, containers, breakpoints, spacing values, responsive reordering, and navigation-open behavior remain pending the approved Figma states.

Prefer normal document scrolling. Scroll snapping or choreographed inner scrollers are progressive enhancements only when the approved experience requires them and keyboard, focus, mobile browser chrome, and reduced-motion behavior remain sound.

## Elevation & Depth

The approved shadow, layering, border, texture, and surface model is not yet documented. Do not infer it from the starter application or recreate the inherited 2025 grain treatment by default. Record exact tokens here only after they are confirmed in Figma or an approved implemented system.

## Shapes

Corner radii, border widths, clipping behavior, and recurring geometry remain unresolved pending the client design system. Do not normalize them to framework defaults or invent a generic rounded-card language.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:

- **Do** preserve Inter and the approved blue/yellow foundations in every proposed design direction.
- **Do** use approved vector logo sources and optimized, rights-cleared raster assets.
- **Do** define visible focus, keyboard, touch, loading, empty, error, missing-image, long-content, and reduced-motion behavior with each component.
- **Do** treat Figma and newer written client approvals as the source for unresolved visual decisions.

### Don't:

- **Don't** treat the Create Next App scaffold or inherited 2025 implementation as the 2026 visual system.
- **Don't** add fonts, gradients, brand colors, motifs, or final component geometry without approved evidence.
- **Don't** hide information behind hover-only interactions or let decorative animation block reading and input.
- **Don't** hardcode unapproved final copy or fabricate testimonials, metrics, partners, links, or institutional claims.
